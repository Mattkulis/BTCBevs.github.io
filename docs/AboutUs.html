Our Story

At face value, we are simply a youth-oriented beverage company, offering a bitcoin-themed refreshment and free bitcoin in each bottle. We aim to meet the demand of consumers interested in new fruity, carbonated soda/pop, riding both the tailwinds of the last crypto-market cycle as well as the headwinds of the next.

Ultimately, Bitcoin Beverages is more than just a beverage company—it's a catalyst for financial literacy and individual empowerment, challenging conventional wisdom and paving the way for a new generation of economically savvy individuals.

Based in Chicago, IL, our line of sweet and citrusy carbonated beverages offers a premium experience that indulges the taste buds while providing a simple and digestible gateway to understanding the complexities of economics and finance, specifically centered around Bitcoin and Bitcoin Adoption.

Our Mission

Through education and innovation, we aim to reshape the way people understand and interact with finance, one sip at a time. Our purpose is the improvement of individual comprehension of financial literacy while simultaneously providing a tenable solution to the issue of fiat debasement.

Our Competitive Advantage

While competitors like Calipso, Jarritos, and Herbert's Lemonade exist in the beverage market, our unique proposition of combining indulgence with the technological zeitgeist distinguishes us from the pack.

Our Business Model

Our business model is straightforward: we sell beverages and give away bitcoin in each drink, supplementing product revenue with ad revenue from partnered ads on the bottle. We distribute our beverages in stores and directly to consumers.

Bitcoin Beverages is not just a beverage company; it's a catalyst for empowerment and enrichment, reshaping the landscape of financial understanding one sip at a time.

Our Roadmap

1. MVP Development

With the awarded funds, we'll develop our tangible and tasteable MVP, leveraging it for media content and initiating grassroots guerrilla marketing campaigns to create buzz and social gravitation around our product.

2. Media Content

Through documenting real customer interactions, we'll generate compelling content, demonstrating the attractiveness and viability of our product. These efforts will attract further investment and retail partnerships, propelling our business forward.

3. Expansion

In one year from now, Bitcoin Beverages (BTC Bevs) will have blossomed into a dynamic ecosystem, seamlessly bridging ideas, people, products, and businesses. With our premium line of carbonated and flavored lemonade beverages, we'll continue to indulge consumer taste buds while serving as a gateway to gaining exposure to Bitcoin.

4. Funding Rounds and Distribution

By this time, we envision having completed our initial funding rounds, launched our products directly to customers, and secured a distributor to expand our reach.
